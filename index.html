How to read this

Model notes
  • Projected nation resultEach fixture awards two points: 2–0 at 60% or more, 1.5–0.5 above 55%, 1–1 from 45% through 55%, 0.5–1.5 below 45%, and 0–2 at 40% or less. The Nation with more total points wins.
  • Unique national rostersEach active roster can be selected only once within a Nation. EuroBowl uses eight coaches and EuroOpen uses four. Both opposing teams may still use the same roster.
  • Classic and SmartClassic reproduces the original raw-rate method. Smart shrinks small samples toward the dataset average, propagates uncertainty through point bands, and reports likely ranges. Switch at any time.
  • Smart priorThe prior has the dataset-wide win/draw/loss proportions and the weight of six games. A matchup with six direct games therefore receives equal weight from direct evidence and the prior; larger samples receive progressively less shrinkage.
  • Fixed boardsIndividual matchups follow fixed board order. The optimizer changes home roster choices only; it never rearranges coaches or boards.
  • Team certaintyThe model combines the active fixtures' win, draw and loss probabilities using exact dynamic programming.
  • Optimizer scopeBest average is a globally exact assignment. Highest win chance and lowest loss risk are broad constrained-search results because those nonlinear objectives are much larger; they are labeled searched rather than guaranteed optimal.
  • Sample strengthLow is fewer than 10 historical games, medium is 10–29, and high is 30 or more. Certainty reflects results, not data quality, so inspect small samples carefully.
  • Meta windowThe weekly feed holds every NAF and FUMBBL Road to EuroBowl game since the final ruleset, bucketed Monday–Sunday. Narrow the window toward EuroBowl to weight the current meta; Meta drift compares it against all weeks since the ruleset. Short windows mean small samples, so Smart mode is recommended.
  • Data recoveryRoster aliases are merged. If one matchup direction is missing, the reciprocal record is reversed and marked as derived. No-data pairings stay neutral.
