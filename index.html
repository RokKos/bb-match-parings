How to read this

Model notes
  • Projected nation resultEach fixture is classified by its score rate. A nation wins when guaranteed wins outnumber guaranteed losses; draws add zero.
  • Nation certaintyThe model combines the observed win, draw and loss frequencies of all eight independent fixtures. It reports the chance of finishing with more actual wins than losses.
  • Sample strengthLow is fewer than 10 historical games, medium is 10–29, and high is 30 or more. Certainty reflects results, not data quality, so inspect small samples carefully.
  • Data recoveryRoster aliases are merged. If one matchup direction is missing, the reciprocal record is reversed and marked as derived. No-data pairings stay neutral.
